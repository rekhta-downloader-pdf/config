# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026

**Rekhta Downloader PDF** extension is built with a strict "local-first" philosophy. Your privacy is paramount, and the extension is designed to operate securely on your own device.

## 1. What We DO NOT Collect

- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect your name, email, IP address, device IDs, or any identifiable user data.
- **No Browsing History:** We do not track what you read on Rekhta or your activity on any other websites.
- **No Passwords or Accounts:** The extension does not require a login and cannot access your credentials.

## 2. What We DO Collect (Anonymous Download Statistics)

To understand which literary works are most popular and to help maintain the extension, we collect basic, anonymous download tallies.

When you successfully finish downloading a **full book**, the extension sends a silent ping containing only:

- The **Book URL** (e.g., `https://www.rekhta.org/ebooks/book-name`)
- The **Book Title**
- The **Date** of the download

**This ping is 100% anonymous.** It contains zero personal identifiers or tracking cookies. If you are using a strict network ad-blocker or happen to be offline when the PDF finishes compiling, the extension will simply ignore the ping and save your PDF normally.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share this anonymous book data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics networks.
